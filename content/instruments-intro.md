## Tagunggu’ Bajau Semporna

Tagunggu’ Bajau Semporna refers to a traditional musical ensemble of the Bajau community in the Semporna district. Based on Cikgu Rosley’s research, Tagunggu’ is a kulintangan ensemble comprising:

- **seven to nine small bossed gongs**, collectively known as the **kulintangan**;
- **two or three large hanging gongs**, known as **agung** or **tawag**; and
- a **double-headed drum** known as the **tambul**.

Together, these instruments form a medium of cultural expression. The ensemble is performed in social and community contexts including customary ceremonies, celebrations, and communal activities among the Bajau of Semporna.

> **Research source:** Rosley bin Hajal, *Repertoir Lagu-lagu Tagunggu’ Bajau di Semporna: Dari Lapangan ke Bilik Darjah*. The description refers to the ethnographic work of Mohd Anis Md. Nor and Hanafi Hussin (2011).
