## Overview

Luvak-luvak is described as a lively titik used for *igal*. Its rhythmic character is more energetic than the other documented forms.

## Dance character

The interview describes an expressive relationship between music and movement: as the playing becomes faster, the dancer may move more slowly and with a more pronounced, graceful curve in the body.

## What still needs confirmation
