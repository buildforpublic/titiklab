## Overview

Tarirai is an accompaniment for *igal*, distinguished by its unique rhythmic character and a steady, moderate tempo.

## Story and movement

The name is linked in oral tradition to a person gathering shellfish at sea who suddenly saw a *taliaga*, described as a snake-like sea animal. Startled, the person lifted a foot; this raised-foot action became part of the dance vocabulary associated with Tarirai.

Tarirai may also be connected with *igal sayau*, an assertive male performance using props such as a sword, spear or shield. Dancers interpret the rhythm in their own style, including the characteristic lifting of a foot.
